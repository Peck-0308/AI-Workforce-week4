# Observations – Use Case 1

## What worked well
The agent responded better to short and direct prompts. The answers were clear, simple, and related to Microsoft Exchange administration.

## Accuracy of responses
Most responses were accurate and gave useful guidance for basic Exchange tasks like mailbox management and distribution groups.

## Did the agent stay within its purpose?
Yes. The agent stayed focused on Exchange administration support and did not go off-topic during testing.

## Areas for improvement
Some responses were too basic and could include more detailed troubleshooting steps or links to Microsoft documentation.

## Overall experience
The activity was easy to complete and showed how Copilot can create an AI support assistant using natural language prompts.
